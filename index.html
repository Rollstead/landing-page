The Problem

Most owners never check their assessment. They just pay the bill.

But assessments are estimates — and estimates can be wrong. If yours is too high, you overpay every year until someone challenges it.

How It Works

1. Send us your address.
That's all we need to start. No research, no paperwork.

2. We run your Assessment Check.
We compare your property's value per square foot against similar properties using the county’s own public data. Within a few days, you'll know whether you have a case.

3. If you have a case, we file the appeal.
We build the evidence and handle the filing. You sign one authorization — that’s it.

The Deadline Is Real

In Minnesota, you have until April 30 to challenge the assessment for taxes payable that year. If you miss it, you wait another year — and keep overpaying in the meantime.
